# HELIOS GRID INDIA — B2B Solar Digital Operating System

A high-performance B2B SaaS landing page designed specifically for the **Indian Solar Energy Ecosystem** and India's **500 GW Renewable Mission**.

The platform connects:
- **Solar EPCs & Rooftop Installers**: PM Surya Ghar subsidy tracking, automated DISCOM net-metering (BESCOM, MSEDCL, TANGEDCO, Tata Power-DDL, BSES), and instant ALMM hardware delivery.
- **Mundra & JNPT Port Stockists**: Liquidity for bonded port inventory at Mundra, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), and Bhiwandi with 0-default ICICI bank escrow and automated GST e-Way bills.
- **Solar Chartered Engineers & CEIG Consultants**: CEIG safety approvals, PVSyst bankability audits (SBI & IREDA accepted), and milestone-secured consulting fees.
- **Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Factories**: Direct ALMM factory-gate pricing, reverse EPC auctions, cutting ₹9.50/unit grid power down to ₹3.15/unit, and Section 32 40% Accelerated Depreciation tax shields.
